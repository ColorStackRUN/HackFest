# HackFest 2026 Design Rules (website version)

Short version of the team's Design Language sheet. If this file and the sheet disagree, the sheet wins. Update this file.

## Voice
- Welcoming first, then proud and professional.
- Short, active sentences. Speak to "you." Name Newark specifically.
- Say "no experience needed." Avoid jargon and hype.

## Colors
| Name | Hex | Use | Text on it |
|---|---|---|---|
| Soot | `#141416` | Header, hero, dark sections | Limestone, Brass |
| Graphite | `#2B2D31` | Cards on Soot | Limestone, Brass |
| Brick City Red | `#A3302A` | Register button, brick motif, one bold word | Limestone |
| Oxblood | `#5E1A17` | Hover states, brick shadows | Limestone, Brass |
| Brass Amber | `#D9A441` | Hairline rules, dates | — (text only on Soot/Graphite) |
| Limestone | `#F3F1EC` | Light sections, body | Soot, Brick City Red |
| Steel | `#8A8F96` | Skyline, dividers, meta on dark | Decorative only on light |
| Passaic Blue | `#3B5B78` | Links, track labels, icons. Sparingly | Limestone |

- Mix: ~70% Soot/Graphite or Limestone, ~20% red, ~5% blue, ~5% brass.
- One accent per section: a red moment OR a brass moment, not both.
- No gradients, no glows, no drop shadows.

## Type
| Level | Font | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Headline | Space Grotesk Bold | Titles. Tight spacing |
| Editorial accent | DM Serif Display Italic | ONE word per section (class `accent`) |
| Body | Inter Regular/Medium | Everything else |
| Tech label | Space Mono, lowercase | Tiny labels like `// 01 / about` (class `label`) |

## Motifs
- **Brick grid:** thin outlines, a few bricks filled red. Not photo-real, not cartoony.
- **Newark skyline:** flat silhouette with smokestacks, at the bottom of the hero.
- **Photos:** real RU-N students and Newark places only. No stock photos.

## Logo
- HackFest 2026 wordmark on top, "GWC x CSRUN" underneath. Use "x", never "&" or "and".
- Sponsor logos: original colors on Limestone, all-white on Soot. Never recolor.

## Website hero spec
1920 x 1080 (16:9). Skyline along the bottom, headline left-aligned.
